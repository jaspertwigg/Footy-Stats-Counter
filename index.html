Corner Box

Place all your pieces. New pieces must touch a corner of one of your own pieces, never an edge of your own pieces. Opponent edges are fine to touch. Fewest pieces left when the game ends wins.

Rules
  • Each player has 21 pieces: every free polyomino shape made of 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 squares joined edge-to-edge.
  • Pieces can be rotated and flipped freely before placing.
  • Your first piece must cover your starting dot.
  • Every piece after that must touch at least one corner of one of your own already-placed pieces.
  • No cell of a new piece may share a full edge with one of your own already-placed pieces.
  • Touching an opponent's piece — by edge or corner — is always allowed.
  • If you have no legal placement, you must pass. The game ends when both players pass in a row (or run out of pieces). Whoever has fewer pieces remaining wins.
